Wakio

Wakio is an iOS alarm app. Set a wake-up time and an AI voice calls you to wake you up. When you answer, your companion talks with you about the morning topics you choose.

How it works

Set an alarm and repeat schedule, pick your companion and topics, and confirm when you’re up to track your streak. Topics can include local weather, calendar events and reminders when you enable them in the app.

Why Wakio uses Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is optional and used only to create your Wakio account and sign you in. It provides your email address and basic profile information, such as your name and Google account identifier. Wakio does not request access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data. You can also sign in with Apple or email.
