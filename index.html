Set your wake-up time. Your Wakey companion calls you, talks with you, and helps you get going.

Your morning, your way

Choose a companion, set an alarm and repeat schedule, and add an optional note about why you want to wake up. Talk naturally when the call arrives. Confirm that you’re up to record your wake-up streak.

A companion you can talk to

Wakey uses Google Gemini Live for real-time AI voice conversations. Sign in with Apple, Google or email to sync your profile, alarms and wake history through Supabase.

Wakey is in development. Calls depend on your connection, device permissions and service availability. Keep an independent alarm for anything important.
