Privacy

Your data in Wakio.

This policy describes Wakio, operated by Harel Meltser. Questions or data requests: harelmtr@gmail.com.

Accounts and synced data

Wakio uses Supabase for Apple, Google and email/password sign-in. Supabase processes your account identifier, email and authentication information. Apple or Google may provide your name and profile information you authorize. Email/password credentials are handled by Supabase authentication.

Your display name, alarm schedules and notes, wake-up history and streak records, and preferences (including your chosen character, personalization and an optional profile photo) can sync to your account. Supabase row-level security restricts normal client access to the signed-in owner’s records. Authorized service administration is separate from these client-access rules. The Cloudflare call service associates your account with your device for calls and scheduling.

Data on your device

Wakio saves schedules, notes, wake history, preferences, optional profile photos and account-specific recovery data on your iPhone. Authentication sessions and device credentials are stored for signing in and connecting to the service. An App Group shares saved alarm, streak and cached weather information with the widget. Signing out clears local account data from Wakio. It does not delete your cloud account.

Voice and typed conversations

Microphone audio, typed messages and relevant conversation context are sent through Wakio’s Cloudflare service to Google Gemini Live to generate replies and carry out requested alarm actions. Context may include your chosen name/personality, alarm note, selected morning topics, streak and weather information. When you enable Calendar or Reminders, selected titles and times may be shared for your briefing. Apple MapKit provides estimates for the commute addresses you enter; the estimate can be included in the briefing. Wakio does not save call audio as recordings. Messages may appear in the conversation interface during use. If Call memory is enabled, up to 20 short summaries can be saved to your account. You can erase them in Call memory. The app asks for permission to share with Gemini in versions that offer the new Privacy & data consent control; you can change that choice in Settings.

Wakio currently uses Gemini’s unpaid service. Google may use inputs and generated responses to improve its products; human reviewers may process this content under Google’s terms. Avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information. See Google’s privacy policy and Gemini API terms. Provider retention and processing are governed by their policies.

Calls and operational diagnostics

Cloudflare and Apple push services process device/push identifiers, schedules, delivery acknowledgments and call metadata to deliver incoming calls and retries. Operational logs and diagnostic traces can include session/device identifiers, build, network type, request endpoint/timing, connection errors, audio-state metadata and delivery history. These are separate from optional product analytics and are used to investigate failures, not to store call recordings. Network providers also process ordinary connection information such as IP addresses.

Crash reporting

Sentry receives crash stacks and limited error categories, device/runtime metadata and app release/build information. The app configures the Supabase user ID as its only account identity field, not your email or name, and filters anonymous installation identities from that field. Screenshots, view hierarchies, conversation text and automatic breadcrumbs are disabled. See Sentry’s privacy policy.

Optional product analytics

PostHog receives a small set of explicit events, such as onboarding completion, sign-in method, alarm edits, call outcomes/first-audio timing, confirmed wake-ups and widget opens. Events can be associated with your Supabase user ID after sign-in; the SDK uses an anonymous identifier before sign-in. There is no session replay, no automatic screen or interaction capture, and no capture of alarm notes, conversation text or audio in product events. Geolocation enrichment is disabled.

Turn off product analytics in Settings → Privacy in Wakio. This disables optional PostHog collection; it does not disable the authentication, calling, sync or operational/crash reporting needed to run and diagnose the app. See PostHog’s privacy policy.

Optional weather and permissions

If you enable weather and allow location, the app requests a location using Core Location and Apple WeatherKit to fetch local weather and sunrise/sunset information. Weather is cached on-device for the widget and can inform your morning conversation. You can disable weather in the app and revoke location or microphone access in iOS Settings. The widget does not independently request your location. Apple’s processing is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

Local records remain until removed or the app’s data is removed. Synced records remain associated with your account unless deleted. Diagnostic and analytics systems retain data according to their configured limits and provider policies; no fixed deletion period is promised here. Providers may process data outside your country. Wakio does not sell your personal data or use these integrations for advertising.

Delete your account or data

Open Settings → Account → Delete account in versions that offer in-app deletion, and confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you may need to confirm again with Apple. You can also contact harelmtr@gmail.com for help deleting your account or making another data request. We may ask you to verify ownership. Never send your password or sign-in token.

The request can cover your Supabase account/profile and wake records, associated call-service records and identifiable diagnostics/analytics under our control. Some provider security records, backups or legally required records may remain under applicable retention policies. To remove device data, also remove Wakio and its widgets from your iPhone (choose Delete App, not Offload App); local credentials may need separate cleanup. Reinstalling or signing out alone is not a cloud-deletion request. Contact us for help and for access or correction requests.
